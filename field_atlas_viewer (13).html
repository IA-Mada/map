Phase 3: Japan Sector

Sunday, Jan 17 – Sunday, Jan 24. Tokyo, Mt. Fuji, 2-Night Kanazawa Basecamp, Shirakawa-go Snow Pass, and Osaka. Hotels booked: Hotel Livemax Shinjuku EAST (Jan 17–19), Hotel Livemax Kanazawa Ekimae (Jan 19–21) and APA Hotel Namba Minami Daikokucho Ekimae (Jan 21–24).

17

Sun, Jan 17: Tokyo, Asakusa to Shibuya Haneda T3, Keikyu Line, Sensō-ji, KIELO Coffee, Sumida Park, Ushijima Shrine, Workman Colors, Honolu Premier, Hotel Livemax, Shibuya Sky, Shibuya Scramble, CoCo Ichibanya

  • 01:00 – 01:45 JST: Land at Haneda Terminal 3. Clear immigration and customs with carry-on only, load ¥3,000 onto mobile Suica/PASMO (or buy a card at the station if your phone can’t), and swap boots for light sneakers.
  • 01:45 – 05:15 JST: Rest and charge your devices in the T3 rest areas while the trains are shut overnight.
  • 05:26 – 06:15 JST: Keikyu Line through to the Toei Asakusa Line (¥610). Trains signed for Asakusa / Oshiage / Aoto / Narita run straight through. Exit at Asakusa (A18) via A2b (elevator) or A5 (stairs).
  • 06:30 – 07:45 JST — Sensō-ji in the quiet: Walk the temple grounds before the crowds, and see Nakamise-dori with the shop shutters still down.
  • 08:00 – 08:45 JST — Neighborhood coffee: KIELO COFFEE ASAKUSA (2-10-5 Kaminarimon, opens 08:00 on weekends). Alternative: Hatoya Asakusa (opens 09:00) for hand-whisked matcha.
  • 08:45 – 09:30 JST — Sumida Park: Follow the riverside boardwalk with the Sunday-morning joggers and dog walkers.
  • 09:30 – 10:15 JST — Across the river: Cross on the Sumida River Walk, the pedestrian walkway under the railway bridge, to the Oshiage bank and the three-arch stone torii of Ushijima Shrine.
  • 10:15 – 11:30 JST — Workman Colors (Tokyo Solamachi 1F): Opens at 10:00. Pick up a windproof fleece or shell and thermal gloves (about ¥1,500–¥3,900) before Monday’s sub-zero Fuji morning.
  • 11:45 – 13:00 JST — Light halal lunch (about ¥700–¥1,400): Honolu Premier Asakusa Hanare for halal ramen (about 20 minutes in and out), or Samurai Soul in Asakusa. Lighter option: salmon/tuna onigiri and a warm taiyaki (check the batter is alcohol-free). Skip the uncertified Nakamise stalls.
  • 13:30 – 14:30 JST: Ride west across the city from Asakusa/Oshiage to Higashi-Shinjuku (about 35–40 minutes via the Asakusa/Oedo or Hanzomon/Shinjuku lines).
  • 15:00 – 15:45 JST: Check into Hotel Livemax Shinjuku EAST at 15:00 (Express check-in). Drop your backpack and shopping bag, and put on a windproof layer for the rooftop wind.
  • 15:50 – 18:30 JST — Shibuya Sky & the Scramble: Take the Fukutoshin Line (about 9 minutes) to Shibuya. Enter Shibuya Sky at 16:15 to catch the 16:52 sunset and the city lights coming on, then drop into the crossing for the Sunday-evening crowd.
  • 19:00 – 20:30 JST — Everyday counter dinner (about ¥700–¥1,200): CoCo Ichibanya Halal Shinjuku Kabukicho (1-19-3 Kabukicho, open 11:00–22:30) for halal curry, about 3 minutes from a mosque for Maghrib/Isha. Confirm it is still open first (see the tip below).
  • 20:45 – 22:00 JST — Konbini staging & sleep: Pick up water, boiled eggs and onigiri at the 7-Eleven/Lawson near the hotel, lay out your Quechua SH100 boots and merino socks, and be asleep by 22:00 for the early start on Monday.
Review (comfort, efficiency, local life): The route runs east first, then west, so you never double back. The 15:00 check-in gives you one proper rest before the evening. The Asakusa morning lets you see Tokyo waking up before the tourist crowds. I put the coffee stop before the Sumida walk, because KIELO opens at 08:00, so there is no backtracking along the river. The airport night and carrying your pack until 15:00 are the least comfortable parts: keep your layers on, and use a coin locker at Asakusa or Tokyo Solamachi if the bag gets heavy.
Check before you go: One halal guide says both CoCo Ichibanya Halal branches closed in 2020, while other lists still show Kabukicho. Look at it in Google Maps first. A backup near Shibuya is Honolu Ebisu, which lists halal certification. Book your Shibuya Sky ticket online for 16:15, because sunset slots can sell out. Confirm the first Keikyu train time and fare on the day.

  • 05:50 JST: Head from the hotel to Busta Shinjuku Terminal (about 11 minutes by taxi, or a walk of roughly 25 minutes; check the route in Google Maps the night before).
  • 06:45 JST: Board Highway Express Bus from Busta Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko Station (~1h 45m, ¥2,200).
  • 08:35 JST: Arrive Kawaguchiko Station. Immediate Fujikyu Railway hop to Shimoyoshida Station.

Shinjuku East Base Note: The hotel is about 2 minutes from Higashi-Shinjuku Station, on the east side of Shinjuku. Plan how you reach Busta Shinjuku the night before so you are not rushing for the 06:45 Fuji bus.

Tue, Jan 19: Tokyo to Kanazawa Basecamp (Night 1) Shinjuku, Hokuriku Shinkansen, Omicho Market, Hotel Livemax, Sushizen Daiki, Kanazawa Castle, Yakitori Ichiban

  • 09:00 JST: Check out of Hotel Livemax Shinjuku EAST (check-out is 10:00; ask them to hold your backpack if needed), then a final streetwear recon & coffee in Shinjuku.
  • 12:00 JST: Board the Hokuriku Shinkansen from Tokyo Station direct to Kanazawa Station (about 2.5 hours, ~¥14,000).
  • 15:00 JST: Arrive in Kanazawa and walk to Omicho Market (about 15 minutes from the station) for fresh seafood bites or Kaisen-don before the stalls close.
  • 17:00 JST: Check into Hotel Livemax Kanazawa Ekimae (Basecamp Night 1, booked Jan 19–21, Non-Smoking, Pay Later).
  • 18:00 JST — Clean Seafood Base: Dinner at Sushizen Daiki for fresh Sea of Japan nigiri, raw Gasu-ebi shrimp, and Saikyo miso-marinated grilled fish.
  • 19:30 JST — Castle Night Illumination Stroll: Walk around Kanazawa Castle Park to see the historic stone walls and gates lit up after dark (the Gyokusen-inmaru Garden light-up only runs on designated days; see the tip below).
  • 20:30 JST — Late-Night Showa Skewers: Head back toward the station to Yakitori Ichiban Kanazawaekimaeten for bincho-charcoal chicken skewers (momo, sasami with salt) and warm green tea.
  • 22:30 JST — Night Wind-Down: Return to Hotel Livemax Kanazawa Ekimae.
Pass Economy: Point-to-point Shinkansen tickets are significantly cheaper (~¥14,000) than purchasing a Hokuriku Arch Pass (¥35,000). Buy individual tickets.
Omicho Market hours: Most stalls open about 9:00 and close around 17:00–18:00, and many are closed on Wednesdays (Jan 20). That is why Omicho is on Tuesday afternoon, which needs the earlier 12:00 Shinkansen.
Castle night light-up: In winter Kanazawa Castle Park normally closes at 17:00. It stays open until 21:00, with the Gyokusen-inmaru Garden lit, only on designated days (usually weekends, holidays and peak seasons). Jan 19 is a Tuesday, so expect to see the outer stone walls and gates lit from outside (they are lit daily until about 22:00) and check the official illumination schedule before you go. You will see the garden by daylight on Wednesday morning.
Food notes: Book a seat at Sushizen Daiki for 18:00. I could not find halal certification for Sushizen Daiki or Yakitori Ichiban. Seafood is generally fine, but ask for no mirin or sake in sauces and marinades (for example the Saikyo miso fish). Yakitori chicken is not halal-slaughtered, and Yakitori Ichiban allows smoking at every seat, so skip it if that matters to you.

  • 21:00 JST — Night Wind-Down: Easy walk back to Hotel Livemax Kanazawa Ekimae to pack for Thursday’s Shirakawa-go and Osaka transit.
Strategic Win: Having Kanazawa locked as a 2-night base camp eliminates hotel check-in/checkout friction completely today and prepares you for a zero-luggage snow run tomorrow.
GYUMON Kanazawa: Open 12:00–22:00 daily. It is pork-free with halal meat and a prayer space, but its halal certification is listed as in progress. A halal restaurant guide lists ¥4,000–¥6,000 per person, so check the menu if ¥3,000 is your limit.
